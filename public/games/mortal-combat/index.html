THE FINAL BOUT

At the Obsidian Gate, two masked champions enter a torchlit stone arena to settle the final bout of an ancient combat tournament. The player controls the azure-clad challenger, whose disciplined strikes and arc-fire technique are the only answer to the crimson champion’s relentless pressure. There is no escape from the ritual circle: victory must be claimed across two rounds, then sealed with one final blow.
