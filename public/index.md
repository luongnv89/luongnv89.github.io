## About

Luong Nguyen (luongnv89) is an Agentic AI & Security Engineer with 15+ years of experience, based in Paris, France. As a Senior Software Engineer at Montimage, he leads development on European R&D projects in cybersecurity, digital twins and applied AI. Three focus areas: **AI agents** (Claude Code, Codex, spec-driven and multi-agent workflows), **agent skills** (authoring, auditing and distributing skills with asm), and **security** (deep packet inspection, LLMs for security, MCP servers, cybersecurity digital twins).

Method: **Intention-Driven Development (IDD)** — every piece of work starts as an intention captured as a structured, agent-ready GitHub issue; his skill set triages, resolves, reviews and ships a tested PR, so the pipeline can run for days unattended. Most of the products below were built this way.
