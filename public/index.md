## About

Luong Nguyen (luongnv89) is a research engineer at Montimage with 15+ years of experience, based in Paris, France. Three focus areas: **AI agents** (Claude Code, Codex, spec-driven and multi-agent workflows), **agent skills** (authoring, auditing and distributing skills with asm), and **cybersecurity** (deep packet inspection, network monitoring, EU research projects).

Method: **Intention-Driven Development (IDD)** — every piece of work starts as an intention captured as a structured, agent-ready GitHub issue; his skill set triages, resolves, reviews and ships a tested PR, so the pipeline can run for days unattended. Most of the products below were built this way.
